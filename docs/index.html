Support

WorldTV+ is a private playlist player for Apple devices. This page explains how to get started and how to contact us when you need help.

Get started

  1. Open Settings → Manage Sources.
  2. Enter a name and the HTTPS URL of an M3U or M3U8 playlist you are authorized to access.
  3. Select Add playlist, then refresh the catalog from Home or Settings.
  4. Select a channel to request playback. Availability depends on the playlist and its provider.

Common issues

  • No channels: confirm that the playlist URL is reachable and uses M3U or M3U8 format.
  • Playback failed: the provider may be offline, geo-restricted, or incompatible with Apple playback.
  • Stale catalog: refresh the source and try again.

Contact support

For questions or reproducible problems, email us with your app version, Apple platform, system version, playlist type, and the steps that caused the issue.

About playlists and content

WorldTV+ does not host, curate, mirror, proxy, or retransmit television content. It starts empty and plays remote URLs from playlists added by the user. Add only sources you are authorized to access. Third-party providers control availability, geographic access, and rights.
