Support

WorldTV+ is a private playlist player for Apple devices. This page explains how to get started and how to contact us when you need help.

Support Privacy policy Disclaimer

Get started

  1. Open Settings → Manage Sources.
  2. Enter a name and the HTTPS URL of an M3U or M3U8 playlist you are authorized to access.
  3. Select Add playlist, then refresh the catalog from Home or Settings.
  4. Select a channel to request playback. Availability depends on the playlist and its provider.

Common issues

  • No channels: confirm that the playlist URL is reachable and uses M3U or M3U8 format.
  • Playback failed: the provider may be offline, geo-restricted, or incompatible with Apple playback.
  • Stale catalog: refresh the source and try again.

Contact support

For questions or reproducible problems, email us with your app version, Apple platform, system version, playlist type, and the steps that caused the issue.

About playlists and content

WorldTV+ does not host, curate, mirror, proxy, or retransmit television content. It starts empty and plays remote URLs from playlists added by the user. Add only sources you are authorized to access. Third-party providers control availability, geographic access, and rights.

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

WorldTV+ is designed to work without accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app does not collect personal data and does not transmit favorites, viewing history, preferences, or catalog cache contents to WorldTV or its developer.

Information stored on your device

The app stores favorite channel identifiers, recently watched channel identifiers and dates, playback and catalog preferences, playlist source URLs added by the user, and a temporary cache of playlist data and channel artwork. This information remains inside the app sandbox on your device.

Network connections

WorldTV+ connects directly to playlist hosts selected by the user, the public iptv-org API only when that source is added, third-party channel and stream servers for artwork and playback, and GitHub when a user opens an About or legal link. Those services receive normal technical request data such as an IP address and user agent under their own policies.

Data sharing, tracking, and children

WorldTV+ does not sell or share personal data, build advertising profiles, track activity across other companies' apps or websites, or include analytics and advertising SDKs. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Live content is supplied by independent third parties and can change without notice.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if WorldTV+'s data practices change. Privacy questions can be sent to hernanrg85@gmail.com or submitted through the public issue tracker.

Content disclaimer

WorldTV+ is an open-source client that plays remote URLs from playlists added by the user through Apple's media frameworks. It does not host, mirror, cache, proxy, restream, sell, or provide access credentials for audiovisual content.

Third-party content

WorldTV+ starts empty. Channel metadata and stream URLs come from M3U/M3U8 playlists added by the user. Users are responsible for using sources they are authorized to access. WorldTV+ is not affiliated with or endorsed by playlist providers, channel operators, broadcasters, trademark owners, stream providers, or iptv-org.

Channel names, logos, trademarks, broadcasts, metadata, and remote streams remain the property and responsibility of their respective owners. Their presence in a playlist does not establish that a stream will remain available, work in every country, be authorized in every jurisdiction, or be endorsed by WorldTV+.

Technical limitations

Remote streams may fail without notice because of downtime, format changes, geographic restrictions, network policies, expiring URLs, or provider-side restrictions. WorldTV+ does not guarantee catalog accuracy, continuity, quality, legality, or fitness for a particular purpose.

WorldTV+ does not implement DRM bypasses, token extraction, location masking, private proxies, credential sharing, or access to deliberately protected content.

Channel-removal requests

Rights holders or authorized representatives can submit a channel-removal request. Include the channel name, source playlist URL, affected stream URL where safe, and an explanation of the rights or authority supporting the request. Do not publish credentials, private tokens, personal documents, or other sensitive evidence.
